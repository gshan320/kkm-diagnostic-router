# Corpus provenance

Where the documents this software reads come from, who owns them, and why none
of them are in this repository.

The Apache-2.0 licence in `LICENSE` covers **only the source code**. It does
not cover the guidelines, protocols or formulary data — see `NOTICE`.

## Why the corpus is not committed

`backend/data/raw_pdfs/` and `backend/data/raw_json/` are gitignored, for three
independent reasons. Any one of them would be sufficient:

1. **They are inputs, not source.** The same category as `backend/data/chroma_db/`,
   which has always been gitignored as rebuildable. The whole index is
   reconstructible from the PDFs with one command.
2. **Their terms are not uniform.** Of the 53 PDFs: 24 explicitly permit
   reproduction with acknowledgement, **1 explicitly forbids it**, and 28 state
   no terms at all in their front matter. Committing the folder wholesale would
   redistribute all 53 as though the most permissive terms applied to every one.
3. **254 MB of binaries.** Git keeps blobs in history after a delete, so this is
   effectively irreversible — unlike the decision to add them later.

Each folder carries a `README.md` with the rebuild procedure.

## Where to get them

**Clinical Practice Guidelines** — the Academy of Medicine Malaysia library:
<https://www.acadmed.org.my/index.cfm?&menuid=67> (109 guidelines, 21
specialties). Download links are of the form `view_file.cfm?fileid=<N>` but are
**Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA-gated and cannot be scripted**. Fetch by hand.

**FUKKM formulary** — regenerated by `python -m app.scrape_fukkm`, which reads
the MOH Medicines Formulary. It maps columns by header text rather than by
index, and refuses to overwrite a good file when validation fails.

**Filenames are load-bearing.** `ingest.py` derives `cpg_title`,
`edition_year`, `edition` and `doc_type` entirely from the filename, so the
convention in README §Ingestion has to be followed exactly:

    CPG <Portal Title> (<N>th Edition) <YEAR>[ v<YYYYMMDD>].pdf
    QR  <same title>   (<N>th Edition) <YEAR>[ v<YYYYMMDD>].pdf

The year is mandatory and must be explicit — without it `parse_edition_year`
falls back to the document text and scrapes whatever year it finds, which once
dated a tuberculosis quick-reference to **2035** from the sentence "TB-free
country by 2035". A rename alone is a reason to re-ingest: `_ground_citations`
resolves page and year against the indexed filename, so 1,174 chunks once
carried a stale name after a file was renamed on disk.

## Copyright owners

Named in the documents themselves:

| owner | documents |
|---|---|
| Malaysian Health Technology Assessment Section (MaHTAS), Medical Development Division, MOH | 18 |
| National Heart Association of Malaysia (NHAM) + Academy of Medicine Malaysia | 4 |
| Malaysian Society of Neurosciences (MSN) + Academy of Medicine Malaysia | 2 |
| not stated in the front matter | 29 |

The typical permissive clause reads:

> The content in this document may be produced in any number of copies and in
> any format or medium provided that a copyright acknowledgement to the owners
> is included and the content is not changed in any form or method, not sold
> and not used to promote or endorse any product or service.

`NOTICE` is that acknowledgement. Note the "not changed" condition: the ingest
pipeline chunks and annotates this content for retrieval, and the annotations
are clearly marked as such in every chunk, but anyone republishing derived
output should satisfy themselves that it meets the condition.

## Two documents that need flagging

**`MTS 2022.pdf` is a third-party re-host, not an official KKM download.** It
carries Studocu watermark furniture on every page — `ingest.NOISE_PATTERNS`
strips seven distinct patterns of it, and the PDF metadata names a phone as the
producer. The clinical content matches Appendix 8 of the Malaysian Triage Scale
(New Revised Version 2022), and the whole triage grid has been transcribed from
it cell by cell into `app/mts_table.py` with page references. **Replace it with
the official KKM copy before any real use.** This matters more than for any
other file in the corpus: MTS 2022 is the document that sets every triage level.

**`MOH Pain Management in Emergency and Trauma Department (2nd Edition) 2020.pdf`
states "No part of this publication may be reproduced or distributed".** It is
the one document in the corpus with an explicit prohibition. Do not redistribute
it, and note that it is also the document whose *analgesia* pain bands differ
from the MTS *triage* pain bands at a score of 7 — see README §Data quality.

## Documents deliberately excluded

* **EMTS_Book.pdf** (Emergency Medicine & Trauma Services Policy, 2012) — over
  a decade old and superseded in practice. Note that the Selangor redirection
  policy cites it as the policy it "shall be read together with", so the
  citation survives in the corpus even though the document does not.
* **A second copy of MTS 2022** circulating as *PROTOKOL MALAYSIAN TRIAGE SCALE
  2022 … PENYERAGAMAN (KKM).pdf* — the same document. Every page of the indexed
  copy carries "Appendix 8 - Malaysian Triage Scale (New Revised Version 2022)"
  as a **running header**; it is not a reference to a larger document that is
  missing.
* **The triage guidelines book launched 27 July 2026** at the MEPS Colloquium is
  not available for public download. Its core guideline is MTS 2022, which is
  indexed.

## Known corpus gap

There is **no MOH primary-care / Klinik Kesihatan protocol** in the corpus. That
is the documented destination of a patient triaged away from an Emergency and
Trauma Department. The Selangor redirection policy supplies *who may be sent
there* — enforced in `app/redirection.py` — but nothing states what care the
destination provides, so "refer to outpatient services" remains unsourced on
the receiving end.

## Non-KKM guidelines (`EXTERNAL_GUIDELINE`)

Indexed **only** where no KKM document covers a presentation, and labelled
wherever they appear: the filename starts `EXT `, the indexed title ends
"(US DoD, not KKM)" (or the equivalent for another publisher), and the UI labels
the type "Non-KKM guideline — international". The model is told that where a
KKM source and a non-KKM one both speak, the KKM source wins; A4.3 ranks KKM
sentences first when it quotes a complication.

| document | why it is here | source |
|---|---|---|
| CHAMP / WHEC Clinical Practice Guideline for the Management of Exertional Rhabdomyolysis in Warfighters, September 2025 | No KKM adult rhabdomyolysis guideline exists. Supplies admission criteria (p5, p25), IV fluid rates (p24, p26), serial labs, NSAID and furosemide avoidance (p30, p26), compartment syndrome (p20, p29). | <https://www.hprc-online.org/resources-partners/whec/clinical-care/clinical-practice> (PDF: `/sites/default/files/document/WHEC_Clinical_Practice_Guidelines_ER_091025_508.pdf`) |

Its PDF text layer carries 438 soft hyphens and words broken across lines
without one; `ingest.clean_text` strips the former and
`ingest.rejoin_broken_words` re-joins the latter (37 joins, each only where the
whole word appears elsewhere in the document and a fragment is not a word).

**Considered and not added (2026-09-30):** *Management of Menorrhagia* (exact duplicate of the
2004 file already indexed) and *Management of Menopause in Malaysia 2022* (no ED use, and its
front matter forbids reproduction: "no reproduction of any part may take place without the written
permission").

**Adult sources searched but NOT added (2026-09-30), pending a decision** - each would be a
labelled `EXTERNAL_GUIDELINE`: Resuscitation Council UK *Emergency treatment of anaphylaxis* 2021
(forbids reproduction without permission), China NCMSA anaphylaxis CPG 2020 (Front Pharmacol 2022,
CC BY), ISBI *Practice Guidelines for Burn Care* 2016, Singapore MOH CPG *Management of Poisoning*
2011. No Malaysian adult anaphylaxis or burns guideline was found openly; hq.moh.gov.my and
www.moh.gov.my refused scripted downloads for two MOH candidates (a primary-care wound-care
guideline, and the 2023 Assistant Medical Officer emergency practice guideline).
